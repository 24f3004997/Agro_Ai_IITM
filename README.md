# Agro AI — Field Force Intelligence

> **AI-guided field force intelligence for smarter agricultural decisions**

## 🏆 Recognition

**Finalist — IIT Madras AgroAI / Syngenta challenge**

Agro AI is an AI-assisted field-force platform designed to help agricultural teams prioritize visits, identify high-value opportunities and recommend the next best field action.

## The problem

Field teams often plan visits using fixed routines even when weather, crop conditions, pest risk, sales signals and local opportunities change rapidly.

Agro AI brings these signals together to support dynamic prioritization.

## Core capabilities

- 🎯 **AI Priority Engine** — ranks villages, growers and field actions by urgency and opportunity
- 🌦️ **Weather & Crop Signals** — incorporates environmental and crop context into recommendations
- 🐛 **Pest Risk Awareness** — surfaces areas requiring attention
- 🗺️ **Route Prioritization** — helps field teams focus limited time on high-value actions
- 💬 **AI Assistant** — provides conversational access to field intelligence
- 📊 **Manager Intelligence Center** — summarizes revenue opportunities, productivity and high-priority actions
- 📱 **Offline-ready workflow** — designed for field environments with unreliable connectivity

## Decision flow

```text
Weather + Crop + Pest + Sales Signals
                 ↓
        AI Recommendation Engine
                 ↓
       Priority / Urgency Score
                 ↓
      Next Best Action + Route
                 ↓
          Field Execution
                 ↓
             Feedback
```

## Technology

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI:** LLM-powered recommendation and assistant workflows
- **Analytics:** field activity, opportunity and recommendation metrics
- **Integrations:** weather, crop and market/field signals

## Product focus

The product is built around one question:

**Where should the field team go next, and what should they do there?**
